The 482 requirements, in short

Your occupation On the Core Skills Occupation List (456 jobs) — or a high-salary role under Specialist Skills
Your salary At least AUD $79,499 a year. Specialist Skills starts at $146,717.
Your experience At least 1 year full-time equivalent, gained in the last 5 years
Your qualifications Must line up with the skill level set for your job. Experience can sometimes stand in.
Your English IELTS 5.0 in every section, or the equivalent. Some people are exempt.
Your health A medical check, health insurance and police certificates

Plus: an Australian employer has to sponsor you.

Updated July 2026. The salary minimums change every 1 July.

That is the short answer. Whether you meet them is the longer one.
