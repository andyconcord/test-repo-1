How it works

Three stages. One team alongside you.

Sponsoring someone on a 482 visa happens in three separate applications, starting with approval for your business to sponsor. We plan all three together, so your first hire isn't held up by the step before it.

5 years of approval, covering multiple hires while you meet your sponsor obligations

01 · The business

Become an approved sponsor

Standard Business Sponsorship confirms your business can sponsor overseas workers.

You

Concord

02 · The role

Nominate the position

You nominate the role and salary, with labour market testing where it's required.

You
Confirm the role, the salary and any job ads.
Concord
Plans labour market testing where required and prepares the nomination.
03 · The person

Your hire applies for the visa

Your candidate lodges their 482 application, linked to your nomination.

You
Introduce your candidate. They answer their questions through a private link.
Concord
Prepares and lodges the 482 visa, then tracks it in your dashboard.

Each stage is a separate application. The Department of Home Affairs assesses and decides each one.

Can my business sponsor?

Four things to get right. We check them first.

Home Affairs decides every application. We'll tell you plainly whether you're ready, and what to fix if you're not.

Book a free consultation or get the free sponsorship checklist
  1. 01

    Lawfully set up and operating

    Your business is legally established and actively trading. Newer businesses can apply too.

  2. 02

    A clean business record

    No adverse information about the business or the people who run it.

  3. 03

    Commitment to local workers

    Fair employment practices, and no preference for overseas workers over Australians.

  4. 04

    A genuine role to fill

    A real position that fits your business. We check it before you nominate.
