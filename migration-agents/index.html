You searched for an immigration lawyer. Here is what you actually need.

In Australia, visas are handled by registered migration agents, licensed by the Office of the MARA to give visa advice and lodge applications. Our Australian team are registered migration agents.

Most visa problems come from small things: applying too late, choosing a visa that does not lead to PR, a partner left off the application, or advice from a forum that was true two years ago.

The good news: you can get a clear first answer, from registered agents, in writing, for free.

What does a migration agent cost? Here is how our pricing works.

Most people searching for a migration agent or immigration lawyer want one thing first: no surprises. So here is exactly how it works with us.

Government fees

Set by the Department of Home Affairs and the same no matter who you use. They depend on the visa and who is included. We will list them for your case in the consultation.

Our fee

One fixed price for your whole case, agreed in writing before we start. No hourly billing. No extra charge for phone calls or emails. If your situation changes, we tell you before anything changes in price.
